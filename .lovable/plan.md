# Align FortuneX around five services

## Overview
Update the existing website without changing its page structure or visual direction. All marketing content will focus only on Reporting Services, AI Automation, Web Application Development, Staffing Services, and Training and Internship. Wording will be shorter, clearer, and easier to understand.

## What will change

### 1. Replace the service catalog
- Remove cybersecurity, penetration testing, maintenance, support, cloud, and other unapproved service offers.
- Keep exactly five service entries, in the requested order:
  1. Reporting Services
  2. AI Automation
  3. Web Application Development
  4. Staffing Services
  5. Training and Internship
- Give each service a clear description, relevant icon, simple offering list, practical benefits, delivery steps, and accurate tools or skills.
- Update the Services page heading from six practices to five services.

### 2. Align every page to the same five-service message
- Home: update the introduction, service strip, service section, benefits, FAQs, and supporting text.
- About: simplify the story, principles, working approach, and service references.
- Careers: replace cybersecurity-focused hiring content with roles related to the approved services, including training and internships.
- Contact: simplify the introduction and contact guidance.
- Header, footer, shared call-to-action band, commitments, and legal pages: remove old service references and simplify wording.
- Keep the existing sections and page structure; do not add unrelated sections.

### 3. Use simpler calls to action
- Replace “Start a project” everywhere with “Talk to us”.
- Replace technical or vague secondary labels such as “Explore capabilities” with plain labels such as “View services”.
- Keep calls to action linked to the existing Contact and Services pages.

### 4. Update SEO and structured content
- Rewrite each public page title and description in clear language.
- Update Open Graph descriptions and page headings where they refer to removed services.
- Add a relevant keywords meta tag to the Home and Services pages using the five approved service names and close search terms.
- Keep structured service data generated from the revised five-service list, so search engines receive the same information visitors see.
- Update FAQ structured data through the revised visible FAQs.

### 5. Verify the result
- Search the full project for removed service claims and old call-to-action wording.
- Check all public pages for correct titles, descriptions, and visible headings.
- Test the homepage and Services page at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation, service links, and layout.
- Confirm the project builds successfully through the platform checks.

## Technical notes
- The shared service data remains the single source for Home, Services, and JSON-LD structured data.
- Existing TanStack routes, components, design tokens, animations, and the current minimalist design will be preserved.
- Legal terms will be simplified carefully without changing their intended meaning beyond removing references to services no longer offered.
